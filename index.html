Apex benchmark analysis

How well do AI models do white-collar work? The Apex benchmark holds 300 real work tasks across three fields (Law, Investment Banking, Management Consulting), each with a correct answer and pass/fail grading criteria. Three models attempted every task, and each answer was graded criterion by criterion against the correct one.
